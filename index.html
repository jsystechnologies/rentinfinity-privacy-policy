Privacy Policy

How Rent Infinity handles information when you use the Rent Infinity mobile application and related owner services.

Last Updated: November 9, 2026
Scope of this policy: This policy describes the current Rent Infinity mobile release, which is intended for property owners. Property manager, tenant, and vendor features are not enabled in this release.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how JSYS Technologies LLC ("JSYS," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and discloses information in connection with the Rent Infinity application ("App") and related services.

Rent Infinity is a property management application that helps property owners review property information, rental activity, expenses, distributions, and financial reports. We take privacy seriously and aim to handle information responsibly.

2. Information We Collect

The information processed may depend on the features you use and the information entered by you or authorized users.

2.1 Account and Contact Information

  • Name, email address, phone number, and other contact details.
  • Account identifiers, authentication information, and account access details.

2.2 Property and Rental Information

  • Property addresses, property descriptions, ownership-related details, and asset information.
  • Lease details, lease dates, renewal information, and rental amounts.
  • Insurance and other property-related information or documents entered or uploaded through supported features.

2.3 Financial and Transaction Information

  • Rent charges and collections, outstanding rent, expenses, payments, and distributions.
  • Financial summaries, cash-flow information, income records, and related reporting data.
  • Other accounting or transaction details entered into the App by you or an authorized user.

Rent Infinity displays and manages financial records. Unless a feature expressly states otherwise, recording or displaying a payment in the App does not mean that JSYS itself processes or holds the underlying funds.

2.4 Device and Technical Information

The App and its supporting services may process technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as device type, operating system and App version, diagnostic information, and log data. The specific information depends on the technologies enabled in the release.

2.5 Files and Documents

Where document or image upload features are available, files you choose to submit may be processed and stored to support property records and related App functionality.

3. How We Use Information

We use information for purposes such as:

  • Creating and managing user accounts and authenticating access.
  • Displaying property portfolios and property-related information.
  • Presenting rent collection, outstanding balances, expenses, and owner distributions.
  • Maintaining lease, insurance, and other property records.
  • Generating dashboards, cash-flow views, income summaries, and reports.
  • Providing support, responding to requests, and communicating service-related information.
  • Maintaining, troubleshooting, protecting, and improving the App and its services.
  • Complying with legal obligations and enforcing applicable terms.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may disclose information in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: To vendors that help operate or support the App, such as hosting, database, authentication, communications, maintenance, or technical support providers, where applicable.
  • At your direction: When you ask us to share information or use a feature that involves sharing it.
  • Legal and safety reasons: When disclosure is required by law, legal process, or is reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, JSYS, or others.
  • Business changes: In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of all or part of our business, subject to appropriate protections.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain account and property records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal, tax, accounting, or regulatory requirements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the circumstances.

7. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also update certain account information through the App, where those controls are available.

To submit a privacy request, contact us using the details in Section 12. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate business, legal, tax, accounting, or security purposes.

8. Account Deletion

If you would like to request deletion of your Rent Infinity account or associated personal information, contact us at the email address in Section 12. We will review and respond to the request in accordance with applicable law.

Deleting an account may not immediately remove all property, lease, transaction, or accounting records associated with that account where those records must be retained for legitimate operational or legal purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

Rent Infinity is designed for property owners and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

10. International Data Processing

Your information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where you live, depending on where our service providers and systems operate. Where required, we take steps intended to provide appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App, our practices, or applicable legal requirements change. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date on this page. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice of material changes.
